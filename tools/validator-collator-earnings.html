Validator Collator Earnings

What a Polkadot validator earns from relay validation alone, and with collating on Asset Hub, People and Bulletin via pallet-validator-collators. A metanode runs the whole Polkadot service layer: relay validation, system chain collation and full nodes, Bulletin storage, RPC and other services. Its cost is one input. The era payout is fixed. Collating creates no new reward. It moves reward between validators.

Network and costs

Per validator, per month

Gross is the validator's whole points share, operator plus nominators. Operator take is the commission, the self-stake cut of the staker pot and the incentive budget share. Net is the operator take minus the metanode cost.

Break-even

My expected monthly operator take as k grows. Dashed line is the metanode cost. Dotted line is relay-only. Hover the curve for the PPS per chain at each k.

k multiplies the PointsPerService of every enabled chain at once. Everything else stays fixed: the era payout, relay points, caps, eligible validators and the metanode cost. More points per block add no DOT. They only move shares between validators, so the average validator always earns the relay-only amount. A higher k can only help a validator that collates more often than average, through a fixed seat or because fewer than N validators are eligible.

Per chain

Gain is my expected extra operator take from that chain over a non-collator.

Formulas and assumptions
  • Drawn validators per chain n = min(cap, K). K is the number of eligible validators, with keys and a node.
  • v of the other collators are validators. Expected overlap with the draw j = n·v / K. A drawn validator that is also an invulnerable or candidate keeps one Aura slot.
  • Blocks per author b = blocks_per_era / (n + others − j). Validator authors m = n + v − j. Points per author era x = PPS · b.
  • My draw probability q = n / K. It is 1 if I am one of the v.
  • Total points P = N·R + Σ m·x.
  • Value per point, gross (pot + w·budget) / P. Operator (pot·(comm + (1−comm)·own) + w·budget) / P. w is my incentive weight over the mean.
  • Relay-only reward (pot + w·budget) / N. A non-collator earns R points. I earn R + Σ q·x points. Monthly is per era × eras per month.
  • Break-even k solves E·V·(R + k·S) / (N·R + k·C1) = M, so k = R·(M·N − E·V) / (E·V·S − M·C1). Here S = Σ q·PPS·b, C1 = Σ m·PPS·b and M is the metanode cost in DOT.
  • My take rises with k towards E·V·S / C1. If that is not above the cost, the break-even is unreachable.
  • The incentive budget is split by weight × points. The other validators are treated as average.
  • Drawn validators author every turn. Every author in the era's validator set earns points, however it got its slot. An invulnerable or candidate that is also a validator earns every era. One that is not a validator earns nothing.
  • One metanode per validator runs all the time. It runs the whole service layer (relay validation, collation and full nodes for the system chains, Bulletin storage, RPC and the other services). The model counts only era points from relay validation and collation against its cost.
  • Not on Polkadot yet: ValidatorCollators and StakingPointsConverter. Cap and PPS default to the Westend values. The metanode cost defaults to 2000 USD per month.
  • Relay points per validator R = relay points per era as read / validators scored. R stays fixed when N changes, and the relay total scales with N.
  • Relay era points have 3 sources. 20 per relay block authored (staking-async ah-client do_note_author, PointsPerBlock). 20 per backer of each included parachain candidate (reward_points.rs, reward_backing). 20 per dispute statement. Approvals and availability bitfields earn nothing.
  • Block authoring gives about 288k points per era, one block per 6 s slot. That is under 1% of the observed ~41.6M. Backing is almost all of it.
  • A validator backs the candidates of the core its group is assigned to. With fewer validators the groups shrink, but each validator still backs about as often. So relay points per validator stay about the same.
  • The observed total fits about 143 backing votes per relay block, for example ~29 candidates × 5 backers. This is an inference, not a chain read.
  • This assumes the number of cores and their use stay the same. If N fell far enough that groups hit their minimum size, points per validator could rise.
  • Sources are the Westend runtime at polkadot-stable2611-rc2. The Polkadot runtime is assumed to use the same values.
